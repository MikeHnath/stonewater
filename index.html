Home/Portfolio/A Sound Estate

Greenwich, CT · 2023

A Sound
Estate

Living with the landscape — not against it

This 4.2-acre Greenwich estate presented a rare opportunity: an existing property with strong bones and a Long Island Sound view that the original design had never fully realized. The owners came to Stonewater with a clear ambition — to create an outdoor environment that felt worthy of the setting.

The result is a complete reimagining of the property's relationship to its site. A 65-foot negative-edge pool now disappears into the horizon at dusk, its edge precisely calibrated to the horizon line visible from the main living area. The bluestone terrace sweeps from the pool to a fully-equipped outdoor kitchen and covered pavilion, all centered on the view.

Below the main terrace, a cascade of graduated planting — native grasses, ornamental shrubs, and seasonal perennials — creates a living transition between the formal hardscape and the natural shoreline. Four seasons of considered planting ensure the property is never static.

Our Services

Pool Design,
Build & Renovation

Water is the most powerful element in any landscape. We treat it that way.

A Stonewater pool is never just a pool. It's the anchor of your outdoor environment — the element everything else responds to. Every pool we design begins not with a catalog of shapes, but with your site: its grades, its sight lines, its relationship to the house and the horizon.

We have designed and built over 300 pools across Fairfield and Westchester Counties — from intimate plunge pools in Westport townhouse gardens to 80-foot infinity-edge installations on Greenwich waterfront estates. No two are alike. Each one is site-specific by design.

Our pool design team includes licensed landscape architects and structural engineers working alongside our construction crews — a fully integrated process that eliminates the disconnection between design intent and built reality that plagues contractor-led projects.

"The pool is not a feature you add to a property. It's the feature the property is organized around."
— Stonewater Design Principle
  • 300+ pools built in Fairfield County
  • 12 APSP design awards
  • Every pool designed by a licensed architect
  • In-house construction — no subcontractors

Every pool. Designed for its site.

Negative-Edge & Infinity Pools

Engineered for properties with elevation or views. The vanishing edge is the most technically demanding pool form — and the most dramatic. We've built over 80 across Fairfield County.

Traditional & Classic Forms

Rectangular, Roman, and freeform pools designed with formal precision. Appropriate for properties where the architecture demands restraint and permanence.

Plunge Pools & Spas

For smaller properties or as a complement to a larger water feature. Designed for year-round use with integrated heating and water management systems.

Pool Renovation

Structural resurfacing, modern filtration upgrades, LED lighting conversions, tiling and coping replacement, and deck renovation for pools that need a second chapter.

Pool Structures & Pavilions

Changing rooms, pool houses, cabanas, and adjacent structures designed as architectural extensions of the home — not afterthoughts.

Smart Water Management

Automated chemical management, remote monitoring, energy-efficient pumping, and solar heating systems that reduce operating costs and extend the season.

About Stonewater

The Stonewater
Difference

Built on 35 years of knowing
Fairfield County's land

Stonewater was founded in 1987 by landscape architect William Hargrove with a belief that the difference between a good outdoor environment and a great one is almost entirely about process — who designs it, how closely design and construction are integrated, and whether the people who build it understand the intent behind every decision.

That belief hasn't changed. What has changed is the depth of our expertise, the sophistication of our design tools, and the breadth of our portfolio — now spanning over 350 completed projects across Fairfield and Westchester Counties.

We are not a general contractor that does pools. We are not a pool company that does landscaping. We are a design-led practice that builds everything we design — which means accountability runs straight through from first sketch to final inspection.

Our Core Commitments

What we believe makes
the difference

01

Design integrity, always

Every engagement begins with a licensed landscape architect. Design is not a service we provide before handing off to a contractor — it's the discipline we apply from first site visit to final punch list. The same person who conceives your project oversees its construction.

02

Site-specific everything

We do not sell packages. We do not reuse layouts. Every project begins with a fresh analysis of your specific property — its grades, its light, its relationship to the house, its soil conditions, its drainage patterns. The design follows from the site, not from a catalog.

03

One team, full scope

From master planning through ongoing property care, Stonewater clients work with one team. No handoffs between design firms, general contractors, and maintenance companies. One relationship, one standard, one point of accountability for the lifetime of your property.

04

Long-term thinking

Fairfield County's best properties are measured in decades, not years. We design for longevity — choosing materials that age gracefully, planting that matures into beauty, and pool systems engineered for decades of operation.

Origins & Milestones

35 years of
building in this county

1987

Founded in Wilton, CT

William Hargrove establishes Stonewater as a sole-practitioner landscape architecture firm, completing 7 residential projects in the first year.

1993

In-house construction begins

Frustrated by the gap between design intent and contractor execution, Stonewater hires its first construction crew — a decision that defines the company's model to this day.

2001

100th pool completed

A Greenwich waterfront estate marks the firm's 100th completed pool installation. The project wins a national APSP design award.

2009

Estates program launched

Estates by Stonewater formalizes the firm's full-property management offering — design, construction, and year-round stewardship under a single relationship.

2018

Second generation joins

Claire Ashford becomes Director of Landscape Architecture, bringing a new generation of design sensibility to the firm's portfolio.

2023

350th project completed

The Sound Estate in Greenwich — a 4.2-acre waterfront transformation — marks Stonewater's 350th completed project and earns the firm's 18th national award.
